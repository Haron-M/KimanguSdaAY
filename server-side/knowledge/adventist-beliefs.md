# Seventh-day Adventist Fundamental Beliefs (28)
1. Holy Scriptures: The Bible is God's inspired Word, the standard of character, test of experience, and authoritative revealer of doctrine (2 Tim 3:16-17; 2 Pet 1:20-21).
2. Trinity: One God in three co-eternal Persons: Father, Son, Holy Spirit (Matt 28:19; 2 Cor 13:14).
3. Father: Eternal Creator, Source, Sustainer and Sovereign of all (Deut 4:35; John 3:16).
4. Son: Jesus is eternal Son, truly God and truly human, born of the virgin Mary, who lived, died for our sins, rose, and ministers for us in heaven (John 1:1-3,14; Phil 2:5-11).
5. Holy Spirit: God, active in creation, incarnation and redemption; inspired Scripture, convicts, renews and empowers (John 16:7-13).
6. Creation: God created the world in six literal days and rested on the seventh, as a recent creation (Gen 1-2; Ex 20:8-11).
7. Nature of Humanity: Made in God's image as a unity of body, mind and spirit, with free will; fell into sin; death is the result of sin (Gen 1:26-28; 2:7; Rom 6:23).
8. Great Controversy: A conflict between Christ and Satan over God's character and law, which Christ will end (Rev 12:4-9; Job 1:6-12).
9. Life, Death, and Resurrection of Christ: His atoning death and resurrection provide the only way of salvation (John 3:16; 1 Cor 15:3-4).
10. Experience of Salvation: Saved by grace through faith, not works; justification, new birth, forgiveness (Eph 2:8-9; 2 Cor 5:17-21).
11. Growing in Christ: Christ gives victory over evil powers; daily growth through prayer, Bible study and fellowship (Col 1:13-14; 2 Pet 3:18).
12. Church: The community of believers who confess Jesus as Lord and Saviour (Matt 16:13-20; Eph 1:22-23).
13. Remnant and Its Mission: A last-day remnant keeps God's commandments and the faith of Jesus and proclaims the Three Angels' Messages (Rev 12:17; 14:6-12).
14. Unity in the Body of Christ: One body from every nation, tribe, language and people (Eph 4:3-6; Gal 3:27-29).
15. Baptism: By immersion, a public sign of faith, death to sin and new life (Rom 6:1-6; Matt 28:19-20).
16. Lord's Supper: Communion with unfermented grape juice and unleavened bread, preceded by foot washing (John 13; 1 Cor 11:23-30).
17. Spiritual Gifts and Ministries: God gives every member gifts to serve the church and others (Rom 12:4-8; 1 Cor 12:9-11).
18. Gift of Prophecy: Manifested in the ministry of Ellen G. White; her writings are a continuing, authoritative source of truth and guidance, but the Bible remains the standard (Joel 2:28-29; Rev 12:17; 19:10).
19. Law of God: The Ten Commandments remain binding; obedience is the fruit of love and salvation, not the means of earning it (Ex 20:1-17; 1 John 5:3).
20. Sabbath: The seventh day (Saturday, sunset Friday to sunset Saturday) is a memorial of creation and a sign of redemption (Gen 2:1-3; Ex 20:8-11; Luke 4:16).
21. Stewardship: We are managers of time, abilities, possessions and the earth; faithful tithe (a tenth) and offerings (Mal 3:8-12; Gen 1:26-28).
22. Christian Behavior: Holy, healthful living; avoiding alcohol, tobacco and unclean foods; modest dress; pure thoughts (Phil 4:8; 1 Cor 6:19-20; Lev 11).
23. Marriage and the Family: A lifelong, monogamous union between a man and a woman (Gen 2:18-25; Matt 19:3-9).
24. Christ's Ministry in the Heavenly Sanctuary: Christ ministers in the heavenly sanctuary; the pre-advent judgment began in 1844 at the end of the 2300 days (Dan 8:14; Heb 8:1-5; 9:11-28).
25. Second Coming of Christ: Literal, personal, visible and worldwide (John 14:1-3; Acts 1:9-11; 1 Thess 4:13-18; Matt 24).
26. Death and Resurrection: Death is an unconscious sleep until the resurrection; the righteous rise at Christ's return (Eccl 9:5-6; John 11:11-14; 1 Thess 4:16-17).
27. Millennium and the End of Sin: A thousand years in heaven between the two resurrections; then sin and sinners are destroyed (Rev 20; 1 Cor 6:2-3).
28. New Earth: God makes all things new for the redeemed to dwell in forever (2 Pet 3:13; Rev 21:1-5).

# Quick facts
- Adventist roots: the Millerite movement; the Great Disappointment of October 22, 1844; the church was formally organized in 1863. Key pioneers: Ellen G. White, James White, Joseph Bates.
- Adventist Youth (AY) is the church's youth ministry; Pathfinders and Adventurers are its clubs for younger members.
- Sabbath School is the weekly Bible study before the Sabbath worship service.
- Well-known Ellen G. White books: Steps to Christ, The Desire of Ages, The Great Controversy, Patriarchs and Prophets, Education, The Ministry of Healing.
